# Acceptable use

x-archive is a personal archival tool for **public** posts on X. It is not a
service, has no server, and sends nothing anywhere except a request to X for the
post you asked for.

## What you may use it for

- Saving a public post's video, audio, or card for your own later reference —
  study, teaching, research notes, a personal library.
- Keeping material you would otherwise lose when a post scrolls away or is
  deleted.

## What it must not be used for

- Private, protected, or otherwise restricted content. The tool does not bypass
  logins and will not be extended to.
- Re-publishing someone else's work as your own, or as part of a paid product.
- Bulk crawling, mirroring, or building a competing archive of X's content.
- Anything that evades rate limits or X's technical measures.

## Copyright stays with the author

The media belongs to the person who posted it. The generated card keeps the
author handle and the source link on its face for that reason. If an author asks
you to stop keeping a copy, stop.
